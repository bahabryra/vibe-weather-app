1. Add a Browser Source.

3. Set it to 1920 × 1080 / 60 FPS.
4. Add your camera as an OBS Video Capture Device.
5. Put the camera above the weather Browser Source.
6. Apply Chroma Key to the camera.
7. Key out green.

The weather scene will appear behind the keyed host.
